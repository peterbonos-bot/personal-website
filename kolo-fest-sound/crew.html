Code of Conduct

Respect • Consent • Inclusion • Accountability

  1. Respect one another
  2. Respect consent and personal boundaries
  3. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated

    Prohibited behavior includes:

    • Sexual harassment, unwanted advances, or inappropriate touching.
    • Unwelcome comments about someone’s body, appearance, relationships, or sexuality.
    • Discriminatory remarks, slurs, or demeaning jokes.
    • Threats, stalking, intimidation, or physical aggression.
    • Repeated unwanted attention or misuse of authority or influence.
    • Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
  4. Help keep the festival safe
    • Consume alcohol responsibly and legally.
    • Do not participate while intoxicated in a way that compromises safety.
    • Follow venue rules and reasonable instructions from festival organizers.
    • Do not engage in illegal activity or behavior that disrupts or endangers others.
  5. Speak up — report concerns

    If you experience, witness, or become aware of harassment, discrimination, inappropriate behavior, or a safety concern, contact any California Kolo Festival board member or designated festival representative.
    You do not have to handle a situation alone.

  6. Accountability

    Violations may result in:

    • Verbal or written warnings.
    • Removal from a class, activity, or festival event.
    • Suspension or exclusion from future events.
    • Removal from volunteer, teaching, performance, leadership, or board-related roles, as applicable.
    • Referral to appropriate authorities when warranted.

    Serious misconduct may result in immediate removal. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

Everyone deserves to feel safe, respected, and welcome.
Respect Every Culture.
Respect Every Boundary.
Look Out for One Another.
