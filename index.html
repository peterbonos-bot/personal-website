About

Peter Bonos is a Bay Area musician and bandleader, concert and festival producer, audio engineer, educator and community arts organizer. He leads Orchestra Euphonos, co-founded Duygu & Friends, performs with Fanfare Zambaleta, and produces community music events including the Berkeley Balkan Bacchanal.
