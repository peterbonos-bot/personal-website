One-to-one study

Lessons are shaped around the individual player. We can work on tone, breathing, articulation, range, reading, phrasing, improvisation, ensemble preparation and repertoire, while keeping the larger goal in view: becoming a more confident and expressive musician.

A practical, musical approach

Peter brings experience as a performer, bandleader, ensemble musician and educator across klezmer, Balkan, Eastern European and related traditions. Lessons connect technique to real music rather than treating exercises as an end in themselves.
