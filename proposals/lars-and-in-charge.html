Booking support

I’d help the band pursue larger festival opportunities and better-paying gigs that fit the live show.

Choose where to pitch

Build a shortlist of festivals, concert series, dance events, and other live performance opportunities. Filter by availability, travel range, and minimum fee.

Prepare pitches and support confirmed shows

I’ll draft tailored pitches for Lars to send, with help from Joe Rosato if needed. The band will handle presenter follow-up, fees, and dates. Once a show is confirmed, I can help with logistics.

California booking requirements

California requires a license and a bond if I book gigs for you. The bond protects the band and starts around $500 a year. For now, I can prep the pitches and you can send them.

Website as the EPK

Build or refresh a mobile-friendly website that doubles as the band’s electronic press kit. Presenters can use one link to see the band live, download press materials, and get in touch.

Listen and book

Feature live clips, upcoming shows, and a clear booking contact.

Press downloads

Make short and full bios, approved press photos with credits, and available tech materials easy to download.

Social media

Give people a reason to follow the band between shows and an easy way to find the next one.

Plan around actual dates

Build a manageable calendar around gig announcements, collaborator spotlights, and new audio or video.

Make posts easy to share

Prepare captions and short edits, coordinate tags with venues and collaborators, and agree who approves and posts each piece.

Belly dance collaborations

Coordinate scheduling with the dancers the band already works with.

Coordinate availability

Check dancer availability against confirmed performance dates and coordinate any rehearsals with the band.

Share the show schedule

Share call times, soundcheck, and set times with the dancers and the band.

Recording and video

Plan audio and video projects that capture what people hear and see at a Lars and In Charge show.

Choose the first production

Decide whether a live performance shoot or a recording session best serves the band’s booking and release goals.

Define what we’ll make

Agree on the crew, budget, and finished materials before the session. For a live shoot, prioritize a full tune that shows the interplay onstage and a short cut that shows the room dancing. Add a dance feature when collaborators are confirmed.

Visual media and branding

Create a recognizable look for the band across posters, the website, social media, and releases.

Set the visual direction

Review the band’s current artwork and agree on typography, colors, photo treatment, and how the band name appears.

Make artwork the band can reuse

Use Photoshop for photo editing and composites, create original illustrations where they fit, and build reusable poster and social templates.

The first 30 days

I propose 4–5 hours of remote work per week in December 2026, then 8–10 hours per week from January 2027 onward.

Week 1

Review the existing EPK, bios, photos, and live footage. Agree on available dates, a minimum fee, and the first venues and festivals to approach.

Weeks 2 to 4

Draft a new EPK using the band’s best existing material, prepare pitches for the band to send, and keep a shared record of outreach.

End of the month

Review the responses and what the new EPK still needs. Decide which opportunities to pursue and which project would help most next.

How we’d work together

If this feels right, let’s agree on December’s priorities and a start date.

Roles

I’d research opportunities and draft materials. Lars or a band member would handle presenter contact and booking decisions.

Preliminary rates

$30/hr for remote work including booking research and pitch drafting, web and EPK design, PR, and graphic design. That’s $120–$150/week in December and $240–$300/week from January onward.
$40/hr for in-person work including production assistance with recording, live sound, and video.

Weekly check-in and invoicing

We’ll check in once a week before I start to agree on priorities and estimated hours. I’ll invoice after the week’s work is complete, with a brief summary of the work and hours.

Additional projects

We’ll agree on deliverables and a budget for each additional project and approve any outside costs before committing.
