About the group

Duygu & Friends approaches traditional repertoire as living music—carried between generations and communities, transformed through collaboration, and meant to be experienced together.

The story

Rooted in Anatolia and the wider Mediterranean, the ensemble draws on Turkish folk and classical traditions alongside music shaped by centuries of movement, exchange and shared cultural memory.

Co-founders

Duygu Gün & Peter Bonos

Duygu & Friends was co-founded by Duygu Gün and Peter Bonos as a flexible home for repertoire they love and for collaborations with musicians from the Bay Area's diverse traditional-music communities. Duygu brings voice and guitar; Peter contributes oud and trumpet, alongside his experience in Balkan, klezmer, Turkish, Eastern European and Mediterranean music.

Repertoire

Across Anatolia & the Mediterranean

The group's repertoire crosses languages and traditions, including music in Turkish, Ladino, Greek, Griko and Italian. The ensemble can move from intimate acoustic songs to a larger, rhythmically driven sound as the instrumentation and occasion change.

Approach

Music as connection

The music creates a meeting place between Anatolia and the Balkans, tradition and contemporary life, family and friends, musicians and audiences, and the many communities that now call the Bay Area home.

A new chapter

Revived with an expanded lineup, Duygu & Friends continues to grow around the music and the people who bring it to life.

Evolution & collaboration

Duygu & Friends has developed through several musical chapters while keeping collaboration at its center.

Earlier formation

Earlier chapters

An earlier formation included vocalist Emmanouela Niniraki. The group later went on hiatus during the pandemic, when Duygu also worked with Jonathan Kipp under the moniker Nakarat.

Guest collaborators

A growing musical circle

Across different performances, the ensemble has welcomed additional musicians and singers, including Marco Ghezzo on violin and Duygu's father as a guest vocalist, bringing a direct family and generational connection to the repertoire.

Revival

The current ensemble

Duygu & Friends is now active again with an expanded lineup, reconnecting the original collaboration between Duygu and Peter with new voices, instruments, repertoire and friendships.

Community roots

The ensemble is closely connected to the Turkish and Balkan communities of the San Francisco Bay Area, where music lives as both performance and social gathering.

San Francisco · Turkish community

Lahmacun Thursday

Duygu & Friends often performs at Lahmacun Thursday at Upcider in San Francisco, hosted by the Gezi Platform. These gatherings place the music among people eating, talking, listening, singing and spending time together.

Berkeley · Balkan community

Berkeley Balkan Bacchanal

The ensemble also performs at the Berkeley Balkan Bacchanal, connecting its Anatolian and Mediterranean repertoire with the Bay Area's active Balkan music and dance community.

Duygu Gün

Musician, artist and community builder.

Background

Music across cultures

Duygu is a Turkish-American vocalist, guitarist, artist and arts professional whose work explores connections among cultures. Her musical life draws from Anatolian and Mediterranean traditions and from the communities she has helped build in the Bay Area.

Community life

Khanka Sam

Duygu lives and rehearses at Khanka Sam, a cooperative community associated with the Dances of Universal Peace community. Duygu & Friends rehearses there as the revived ensemble develops its repertoire and sound.
